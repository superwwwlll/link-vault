Reading font in this app
=======================

Latin, digits, and punctuation already present in OpenSI Serif stay OpenSI Serif.
Source: https://github.com/auraunii/opensi-fonts/releases/download/release/OpenSI-Serif-and-Mono.zip
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL-OpenSI-Serif.txt and FONTLOG-OpenSI-Serif.txt.
This is not an Anthropic or Claude font.

Han characters, and Chinese punctuation OpenSI does not include, come from
Zhuque Fangsong (technical preview) v0.212:
https://github.com/TrionesType/zhuque/releases/download/v0.212/ZhuqueFangsong-v0.212.zip
Copyright 2023 JadeFoci. License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL-ZhuqueFangsong.txt.
The combined file is not named Zhuque. It is not a Claude official Chinese font.
GB2312 Han in that release are included. Symbols and rare characters it does not
contain fall back to the device font.

OpenSI Mono is not bundled. Its download states no extra license.
